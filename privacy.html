TWIRVALL Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

TWIRVALL is an app for motorcyclists: it lets you create and share routes, join communities, chat, keep track of your bike's servicing, and buy and sell gear with other riders. This policy explains what data is collected, why, and what you can do about it.

Data controller: TWIRVALL is a personal project, maintained by an individual.
Contact: twirvall@gmail.com

How long we keep it

Your account data is kept for as long as the account exists. When you delete your account, personal data is removed immediately (see the next section). Supabase backups may retain data for a limited additional period before being overwritten.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time, inside the app, under Rider → Delete account. You do not need to ask us for anything.

When you delete your account:

  • Deleted: email and sign-in (including the link to Apple or Google), name, bio, photo, phone, motorcycles, servicing records, emergency contacts, marketplace listings, shared locations, recorded trips, notification identifier and app language.
  • Not deleted: content you posted in shared spaces — posts, messages, comments, routes, groups you created, and places, place reviews and place photos. That content stays, attributed to a removed user instead of your name.

If you created your account with Apple, deleting it asks you to confirm with Apple and revokes TWIRVALL's access to your Apple account. With Google, you can also remove access at myaccount.google.com → Security → Third-party apps & services.

The reason is straightforward: deleting your messages would delete half of other people's conversations, and deleting a group you created would take every member's history with it. If you want specific content removed beyond this, write to the contact address above.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict processing, object to it, and receive a copy in a readable format. Correction and deletion are available directly in the app; for the rest, use the contact address above.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority. In Portugal, that is the Comissão Nacional de Protecção de Dados.

Security

Access to data is controlled at the database level by policies that ensure each user only reads what concerns them. Communications are encrypted. Group and chat photos are held in private storage, reachable only through temporary links generated on demand.

Minors

TWIRVALL is not intended for anyone under 16. If we become aware of an account belonging to someone under 16, it will be deleted.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes significantly, the change will be announced in the app before taking effect. The date at the top shows the last revision.
